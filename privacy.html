1. The short version

The Extension works inside your own browser. Your contact lists, messages and reports are stored only on your computer. We do not upload them to our servers, we do not sell them, and we do not use them for advertising.

When you press "Start sending", the message and recipient number are passed to WhatsApp Web in your own browser tab, and WhatsApp Web sends them through your own WhatsApp account. That sending is governed by WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your WhatsApp chats, contacts list or account details.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking inside the Extension.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not read pages other than WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com).

5. Your contacts' data is your responsibility

The phone numbers and names you upload belong to your customers. You are the "data fiduciary" or "data controller" for that data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the EU General Data Protection Regulation. You must have their consent to message them, and you must stop messaging anyone who asks you to stop. The Extension's do-not-message list helps you do this.

6. How long data is kept

Data stays in your browser until you press "Clear campaign" or remove the Extension. Removing the Extension deletes all its stored data.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold your Extension data on our servers, you control it fully: you can view, export ("Download report") or delete it ("Clear campaign" or remove the Extension) at any time. For anything else, or for questions about data we hold through our website or support emails, write to [support email]. We reply within 30 days. EU users may also complain to their local data protection authority; Indian users may contact the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

The Extension is for businesses and adults. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

9. Security

Data stays inside your browser's protected extension storage. Keep your computer and browser secure, because anyone with access to your browser profile can open the Extension.
