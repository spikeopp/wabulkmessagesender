Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan sends up to 50 personalized messages a day with full reports. No card, no trial timer.

Can my WhatsApp number get banned?

Any tool that sends many messages carries some risk, and no one can promise zero risk. The Safety Meter, daily limit, new-number mode and message gap lower it a lot. The most important rule: only message people who agreed to hear from you, and always include a way to stop.

Do I need to save numbers in my phone?

No. Upload a CSV or paste numbers with the country code. The extension opens each chat for you.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes, it works with both the normal and the Business app, through WhatsApp Web on your computer.

Where is my contact list stored?

Only in your own browser. We never upload your contacts or messages. Removing the extension deletes everything.

Do I need to keep the computer on?

Yes, while sending. Keep the dashboard tab and the WhatsApp Web tab open. If you close them, press Continue later and it starts from where it stopped.
